In mid-2026, Swedroe highlighted Djellal Djouad’s *Convergent Faults* paper to warn investors about the systemic risks brewing in the private credit market. \[[1](https://larryswedroe.substack.com/), [2](https://larryswedroe.substack.com/p/private-credits-convergent-faults)\] Djouad’s work argues that the private credit market has reached a configuration where the stress trajectory carries much higher, synchronized systemic risk than the consensus is willing to price in. Swedroe uses this empirical research to educate investors on the hidden, underlying risks associated with chasing yield in the private debt space.
